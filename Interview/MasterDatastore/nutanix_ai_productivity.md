# Nutanix AI & Engineering Productivity

**Core Competencies Demonstrated:** Engineering Leadership, AI Implementation, Process Automation, Change Management
**Best Used For:** "Tell me about a time you improved efficiency", "How do you use AI practically?", "How do you manage engineering teams?"

---

## 1. Situation (Context)
At Nutanix, I was the Engineering Manager leading a team of 12 engineers. The core challenge we faced was two-fold: we needed to ensure the engineering team was fully enabled with modern tools, and management needed a direct, transparent line of sight into project deliverables and execution without bogging developers down in administrative work.

## 2. Task (The Challenge)
My objective was to dramatically increase the team's coding output while simultaneously solving the persistent issue of poor project tracking. Developers inherently dislike creating Jira tickets and maintaining project boards as it takes them away from deep work. I needed to find a way to automate the tracking and accelerate the actual coding.

## 3. Action (What YOU Did)
I tackled this from two angles—enablement and automation:
- **AI Enablement:** I championed the introduction of AI coding tools for my team. I secured budget and approval from upper management. To drive actual adoption and avoid being "delusional about the impact," I personally used the tools extensively in my own workflow. This allowed me to guide the team accurately on best practices and limits.
- **Process Automation:** To solve the tracking issue, I introduced an automation tool that linked directly to developer code commits. It automatically created Jira tickets and updated the boards based on the code being pushed. I also built custom internal tools to reliably track the productivity metrics of my team without requiring manual input.

## 4. Result (The Outcome)
The implementation was a massive success. The automation reduced the time spent on project tracking and management by 80%, completely removing the administrative burden from the engineers. Furthermore, the mandatory, guided adoption of AI coding tools improved overall team productivity and output by 30%. I proved that practical AI tooling and workflow automation can directly impact the bottom line of engineering cost-to-serve.
