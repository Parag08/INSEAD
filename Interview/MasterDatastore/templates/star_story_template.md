# Story Title

**Core Competencies Demonstrated:** (e.g., Cross-functional leadership, Crisis management, Analytical thinking)
**Best Used For:** (e.g., "Tell me about a time you failed", "How do you handle difficult stakeholders?")

---

## 1. Situation (Context)
*Set the scene. Keep it brief but relevant.*
- What was the overarching context?
- Who were the key players?

## 2. Task (The Challenge)
*What was your specific objective or the problem you needed to solve?*
- What were the constraints (time, budget, resources)?
- Why was this important to the business?

## 3. Action (What YOU Did)
*This is the most important part. Focus on "I", not "We". Describe your specific actions, decisions, and strategies.*
- How did you approach the problem?
- What roadblocks did you face and how did you navigate them?

## 4. Result (The Outcome)
*Quantify the impact whenever possible. Share what you learned.*
- What were the exact results? (metrics, revenue, time saved)
- What was the feedback?
- What would you do differently next time?
