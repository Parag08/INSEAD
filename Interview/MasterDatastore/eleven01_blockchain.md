## 1. Situation (Context)
As a founding member and Blockchain Developer at Eleven01, we were tasked with providing scalable, secure decentralized solutions for enterprise businesses.

## 2. Task (The Challenge)
The business required a robust, foundational MVP that could handle enterprise demands better than a traditional database. I needed to build this from the ground up while simultaneously scaling the technical organization.

## 3. Action (What YOU Did)
I architected the foundational blockchain MVP and defined the strategic product roadmap. To ensure we could execute, I led a 10+ member cross-functional team. Instead of just focusing on the code, I established the core cultural values and operational standards necessary for the technical team to scale and focus on delivering tangible differentiation.

## 4. Result (The Outcome)
By aligning our technical architecture directly with our business objectives, we successfully developed multiple differentiating features for the blockchain. This execution culminated in a successful Initial Coin Offering (ICO), proving my ability to take complex, frontier infrastructure and drive it to a major liquidity milestone.
